WHO WE ARE

From lighting concepts to delivery.

LAITEC is a lighting engineering company based in Saint Petersburg, Russia, established in 2012. Its project portfolio spans sports venues, architectural environments and cultural facilities. The team works across lighting concepts, calculations, design documentation, equipment supply, installation and commissioning.
